ASTER Reader

This reader supports ASTER (L1T) Level 1 Precision Terrain Corrected Registered At-Sensor Radiance of collection version '003' and '031'. VNIR, SWIR, and TIR data bands are read if they are available and the raw digital number are scaled to radiance values.
The band names are as follows

VNIR_BAND_1
VNIR_BAND_2
VNIR_BAND_3N
 SWIR_BAND_4
SWIR_BAND_5
SWIR_BAND_6
SWIR_BAND_7
SWIR_BAND_8
SWIR_BAND_9
 TIR_BAND_10
TIR_BAND_11
TIR_BAND_12
TIR_BAND_13
TIR_BAND_14
