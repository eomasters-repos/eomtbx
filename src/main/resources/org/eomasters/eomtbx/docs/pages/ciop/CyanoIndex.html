Cyanobacteria Index (CI)

The Cyanobacteria Index (CI) is a tool used for the satellite detection of cyanobacteria blooms in water bodies. Cyanobacteria-dominated blooms are harmful due to their toxicity and impact on marine ecosystems, thus it is important to monitor them. CI uses bands near 680 nm, and relies on the weak Chl-a fluorescence of cyanobacteria. The Cyanobacteria Index is particularly useful because it does not require comprehensive atmospheric correction, making it effective even in challenging conditions. But it is suggested to provide reflectance values and correct the data for the Rayleigh scattering.

CI is based on the reflectance spectral shape, focusing on wavelengths around 680 nm. It uses the relative depth of the local minimum of the reflectance curve caused by cyanobacteria blooms.

Input Requirements

The source product must provide one band in each of the three spectral ranges. A center band, a lower band, and an upper band.

Band Min. Wvl Max. Wvl
Center 675 nm 685 nm
Lower 660 nm 670 nm
Upper 700 nm 750 nm
